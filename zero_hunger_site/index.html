AI / ML: surplus forecast

Exponential smoothing on weekly surplus (meals) predicts next week's supply so NGOs and volunteers can plan pickups ahead.

How the matching score works

score = w1 × proximity + w2 × urgency + w3 × fit

  • Proximity: closer NGOs score higher (distance by the haversine formula).
  • Urgency: how badly the NGO needs food right now.
  • Fit: whether capacity covers the quantity and the food type is accepted.
  • Hard filter: NGOs that cannot be reached before the food spoils are excluded.

This is an explainable weighted-scoring baseline. With real data, the weights can be learned (for example by logistic regression on accepted vs. rejected matches) and the forecast replaced with ARIMA or a gradient-boosted model.
